**Method:** Public, logged-out Facebook page review. No login, messaging, reactions, comments, follows, or other account actions were used.

## Coverage boundary

**FACT —** Every shortlisted alternative with a representative Facebook page was checked: StoreX, Zatiq, Sellsull, SeloraX, FrameX Tech, ShopZero, Shopify, WooCommerce, and Daraz Bangladesh. The manual Facebook/Instagram + chat + spreadsheet alternative has no single representative page. The Bitcommerz page linked from its website was also rechecked as a watchlist item.

**FACT —** Facebook's logged-out surface exposed between one and three current posts for most pages before a login wall. Therefore, exact 30-day and 90-day post totals, complete pillar shares, and true best/worst rankings are **not available** from this method. Missing engagement totals are recorded as “not displayed,” never as zero.

## Direction for BizTrackBD

**INFERENCE —** The strongest reusable pattern in the local sample is problem-first Bangla content tied to an operational job: fake-order loss, abandoned-cart recovery, fragmented order/payment/delivery work, and AI-assisted moderation. Generic category-superlative copy is less distinctive.

**INFERENCE —** BizTrackBD should compete with short proof-led demonstrations: one pain, one screen, one measurable outcome, and one CTA. Primary measures should be demo opens, store publishes, first orders, and qualified merchant conversations—not follower count or raw reactions.

**ASSUMPTION TO TEST —** A weekly mix of two pain-led reels, one product walkthrough, one objection-handling post, and one merchant-proof update should outperform broad “all-in-one” promotion if each asset links to a trackable demo or activation event.
