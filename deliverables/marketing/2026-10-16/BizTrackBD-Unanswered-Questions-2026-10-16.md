# BizTrackBD unanswered questions

Observed 16 October 2026. These items could not be answered reliably from public data.

## Ask the BizTrackBD team

1. Which production workflows have current end-to-end evidence: courier booking/tracking, Bangla SMS, abandoned-cart SMS, Meta CAPI, custom domains/SSL, POS, and cross-store trust scoring?
2. Can a new merchant complete a live subscription payment by card today? What operational event counts as “instant activation”?
3. What were the last 30 days of visits, demo opens, signups, published stores, first real orders, paid conversions, and churn—by source?
4. How many founding merchants can the team onboard per week without reducing support quality?
5. Which data can be imported from Facebook-only selling, Shopify, WooCommerce, or spreadsheets, and who cleans it?
6. What are the final plan limits, overage rules, refund terms, and fair-use conditions?
7. Who owns the public-claim register, and what evidence and expiry date are required before a claim is published?

## Ask merchants

1. Walk me through the last order that went wrong. Where did the information live, and what did the mistake cost in courier fees, time, blocked stock, or lost trust?
2. What would stop you from moving from inbox/spreadsheet selling to a proper store: setup time, price, learning, migration, courier reliability, or something else?
3. Which single result would make you pay after a free trial? What would make you cancel in the first month?
4. Would you prefer self-serve setup or a guided launch? What help would you expect, and how quickly?
5. May we measure a before/after baseline and publish it? Which details must remain private?

## Ask competitors’ customers

1. What did the product do well enough that you kept paying?
2. Which promised workflow needed manual work, extra tools, or support?
3. What was the full first-year cost after setup, add-ons, overages, staff time, and migration?
4. How long did it take to publish the store and receive the first real order?
5. How did support respond when payment, courier, domain, or campaign tracking failed?

## Recheck publicly in 30 days

- Competitor prices, offers, limits, active Meta ads, and public Facebook cadence.
- Bitcommerz’s social identity and newer entrants such as StoreKoo.
- BizTrackBD’s mobile overflow, card/payment wording, proof-sensitive claims, and first measured acquisition cohort.
