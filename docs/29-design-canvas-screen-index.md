## Reading the canvas

- Shared components (Sidebar, Topbar, MobileTabs, SettingsNav, WizardPreview, MktHeader, MktFooter, SaSidebar) are imported by other artboards. Change them once.
- The design is a reference for layout, hierarchy and copy. Final components come from `packages/ui` (see docs/09).
- Demo data is deliberately Bangladeshi: BDT, Dhaka districts, bKash/Nagad TrxID, Pathao/Steadfast/RedX.
- Plan limits shown: Starter 300 products and 500 orders a month, Pro 2,000 and 3,000, Business unlimited. Treat prices and limits as hypotheses to test (docs/05).
