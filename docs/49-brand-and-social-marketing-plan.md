## 4. Brand guidelines (starting draft for the branding manager)

### Personality: the sharp business partner
Knows the numbers, protects your margin, speaks plainly, never talks down.

### Voice attributes
| Attribute | We are | We are not | Sounds like | Does NOT sound like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sharp | Specific, number-led, practical | Vague or hype | "Ekta refused parcel-e ৳140 jay. 10-ta hole ৳1,400." | "Revolutionary e-commerce solution!" |
| Plain | Everyday Bangla/English, no jargon | Dumbed down | "Order aslo, bKash TrxID check korun, courier book korun." | "Leverage our omnichannel fulfilment stack." |
| Protective | Calm, on your side | Fear-mongering | "Risky order hold kora ache. Ager ekbar call korun." | "Fake customers will destroy your business!" |
| Honest | Say what is live and what is coming | Overpromise | "Eta ekhon beta-te. Apnar feedback chai." | "100% guaranteed results." |

### Tone by channel
| Channel | Tone |
|---|---|
| Facebook / groups | Warm, casual Banglish, short, helpful answers, light emojis |
| Reels / TikTok | Quick, punchy, problem in the first 2 seconds, on-screen Bangla captions |
| YouTube | Patient step-by-step how-tos, calm |
| LinkedIn | Professional English, founder/product story and numbers, for investors and agencies |
| SMS / email | Short, direct, bilingual |
| Support | Empathetic, blame-free, fast |

### Language rules
- Banglish: Bangla words in Roman script are fine for social. Use Bangla script for SMS, email, help articles and anything with steps.
- Product words stay in English: order, courier, bKash, TrxID, store, domain.
- Numbers in taka with the ৳ sign (৳1,499). No dollar sign except in Shopify comparisons.
- Never promise a result. Use "up to", show the calculation, and date every comparison.
- Avoid: "best", "number 1", "guaranteed", "100%", unproven percentages, naming merchants without written consent.
- Inclusive, gender-neutral language for unknown people.

### Visual direction (brief only; the manager proposes two options)
- Reference energy: bKash and Pathao. Bold color, big friendly type, rounded shapes, instantly local.
- Current repo colors for reference: navy `#1A1A4E`, violet `#5B4FE8`, green `#15803D`. Option A keeps and brightens them. Option B moves to a fresh palette.
- Typography: one display face plus a Bangla-capable body face (Hind Siliguri is already in use). Every font must be licensed for commercial use.
- Imagery: real screens, real parcels and phones, real sellers. Avoid stock photos of Western offices.
- Logo: symbol + wordmark with a storefront motif. Needs to work at 32 px (favicon, avatar), in one color, in dark and light, and with a Bangla lockup.

### Logo brief (deliverables)
1. Two concept directions with rationale.
2. Chosen direction: primary, stacked, symbol-only, one-color, dark-background versions.
3. Social kit: profile pictures, cover images for Facebook, YouTube and LinkedIn, Reel cover template, post templates (quote, number, how-to, before/after).
4. Brand guideline PDF: logo rules, colors with codes, type, voice, do/don't, example posts.
5. Delivery: editable files (Figma or Canva), exports, font licences.

## 5. Merchant sign-up funnel

Visit → demo admin (no signup) → start (4-step wizard) → store seeded with demo data → publish → first order within 72 hours → paid plan.

| Step | What to measure | First target (ASSUMPTION; adjust after 30 days) |
|---|---|---|
| Visit → demo admin | Click rate | 15% |
| Demo → signup | Rate | 10% |
| Signup → published store | Rate | 50% |
| Published → first real order in 72h | Rate | 25% |
| Trial → paid | Rate | 15% |

**Founding merchant offer (launch deal):** limited to 20 stores, one free month or half price for 3 months, in exchange for: written case-study consent, one screenshot of their dashboard after 30 days, and one 5-minute feedback call. This is how you get named proof. Write the offer terms down and keep the signed consent (a Google Form is enough).

**Getting the first 20 (all four routes you picked):**
1. **Facebook seller groups:** join, answer questions for 2 weeks with no pitch, then offer free store setup to 10 sellers who ask.
2. **Personal network:** list 30 sellers you know, message each personally this week, set up their store on a call.
3. **Direct outreach:** find 100 Facebook page sellers with frequent COD posts; send a short voice note or message offering a free 15-minute setup.
4. **Agencies/freelancers:** offer a partner price and a listing on a "Build with BizTrackBD" page; one agency can bring 5 to 20 stores.

## 6. Social media plan: first 90 days (budget ৳10,000 to ৳50,000 a month)

**Rule:** Facebook is the home; Reels and TikTok use the same videos; YouTube holds the long how-tos; LinkedIn is for the founder/product story and investors, posted weekly, not daily.

### Phase 1: Weeks 1 to 4. Foundation (organic, ৳5,000 to ৳10,000 on tools and boosting)
- Register company, secure handles, set up Facebook page, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, WhatsApp Business.
- Brand kit v1 from the manager. Pinned post, page bio, link tree with the demo admin link.
- Record the hero video: 60-second "order arrives → verify bKash → book courier → print label".
- Launch the founding-merchant offer to personal network and groups.
- Output: 12 posts, 8 short videos, 2 how-tos, 5 merchants onboarded.

### Phase 2: Weeks 5 to 8. Test the three pillars (৳20,000 to ৳35,000)
- Two weeks per pillar, equal budget, same audience (page sellers in Bangladesh, 22 to 45).
- Meta Pixel and Conversions API must be tested before any paid spend (PROOF NEEDED).
- Every ad sends to the demo admin first, not the signup form.
- Output: winner pillar chosen on cost per signup, 10 founding merchants.

### Phase 3: Weeks 9 to 12. Prove and scale (৳30,000 to ৳50,000)
- Turn the first merchants into case studies (with consent): "X store, 30 days, Y orders, Z parcels saved".
- Retargeting for demo-admin visitors and half-finished signups.
- Open the agency partner page and a simple referral reward.
- Output: 20 merchants, 3 case studies, a measured funnel.
