Rules that keep it from looking generated

Flat and bordered
No gradients, glows, blur or colored shadows. Structure comes from 1px lines and spacing.
Real data, local voice
Every mock uses BDT amounts, Bangla names, Dhaka districts, bKash and Pathao. No lorem ipsum, no "John Doe".
Say what it does
Buttons are verbs. Headlines name an outcome. Icons sit beside a label, never alone in a decorative circle.
