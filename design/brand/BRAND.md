## Type
Headlines and wordmark: Baloo Da 2, 800. Body: Hind Siliguri, 400 and 600. Both SIL OFL, cover Bangla and English.

## Logo
Growth b: white "b" with a marigold arrow on a deep green rounded square. Wordmark is lowercase `biztrack.bd`, ".bd" marigold on dark and dark gold on light. Lockups: primary, stacked, symbol (app icon and open), on dark, one colour and reversed, Bangla (`বিজট্র্যাক বিডি`).
- Clear space: the arrow's height on every side. Smallest: symbol 16 px, wordmark 20 px tall.
- Never: recolour the arrow green, stretch, outline, or put on a photo without the square.
- Running text and page titles use `BizTrackBD`. The lowercase wordmark appears only in the logo.

`symbol-tile.svg` (app icon), `symbol-open.svg` (no square, for light grounds), `symbol-mono.svg`, `symbol-mono-reversed.svg`. Wordmark is live Baloo Da 2 text in `apps/marketing/src/components/brand-logo.tsx` (not outlined; outline it in a design tool before using as a standalone file).
